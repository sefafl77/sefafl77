# Asset sources

- `dirbustersync-header.svg`: original vector artwork using the charcoal and warm paper palette from [PenSefter](https://pentest.sefafl.online/). The persona is Lemmings from *Dead Mount Death Play*, confirmed by the account owner. “The Monster” is the [official episode 5 title](https://www.crunchyroll.com/watch/G4VUQ87NZ/the-monster), not an attributed line of dialogue. The ellipsis is an original design element. [Official anime character page](https://www.dmdp-anime.jp/character/).
- `htb-profile-latest.png`: latest screenshot provided by the account owner on 16 October 2026. Season 12 position is #109. Metrics describe that screenshot, not a live feed.
- `security/splunk.svg` and `security/elastic.svg`: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), CC0; brand trademarks remain with their respective owners.
- `security/wazuh.svg`: Wazuh wordmark embedded on the [official Wazuh website](https://wazuh.com/).
- `security/crowdstrike.svg`: [official CrowdStrike Falcon asset](https://www.crowdstrike.com/content/dam/crowdstrike/marketing/en-us/images/graphics-and-illustrations/marketplace/CS_Logo_Falcon.svg).
- `security/sentinelone.svg`: [official SentinelOne website header asset](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt53c99b43892c2378/blt5123292dc93d5b2c/68c19611d8ada2430d2f34ab/header-logo-dark.svg).
- `security/datadog.svg`, `security/opentext.svg`, and `security/trendmicro.svg`: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), CC0. ArcSight is represented by its parent OpenText's wordmark; Vision One is represented by the Trend Micro mark, with the product name alongside it.
- `security/microsoft-sentinel.svg`: [Microsoft's Azure-Sentinel repository logo asset](https://github.com/Azure/Azure-Sentinel/blob/master/Logos/Azure_Sentinel.svg).
- `security/*-card.svg`: original cream card layout containing the corresponding logo. The section lists product interests, not certifications or vendor affiliations.

Product classifications are linked directly in the README to the vendors' product pages.
